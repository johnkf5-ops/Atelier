# CV Submission Form — Written Summary

This is the 100–200 word summary to paste into the Cerebral Valley submission platform's "Written summary" field. Not part of the public repo's positioning — purely for the form.

---

## Recommended copy (188 words)

**Atelier — an AI art director for working photographers.**

Every working fine-art photographer spends roughly thirty percent of their time on applications and most of those go to opportunities they were never going to win. Discovery is scattered, fit is opaque, and every application package takes six to ten hours.

Atelier reads your portfolio with vision, builds a structured Artist Knowledge Base through a short interview, then runs a long synchronous pipeline of six specialist agents on Claude Opus 4.7. Two run on Managed Agents (Opportunity Scout + Rubric Matcher); the rest are direct SDK calls. The Rubric Matcher is the novel core: it pulls each opportunity's past-recipient portfolios, sends them as image content blocks alongside the artist's own work, and scores aesthetic fit with reasoning that cites specific images and named recipients. Programs that don't fit get filtered out with explicit "why not" reasoning — saying no with reasons is the product.

The artifact is a Career Dossier — printable PDF and web view — with ranked opportunities and submission-ready statements, proposals, CVs, and cover letters in the institutional voice each program expects.

## Alternate shorter version (135 words)

**Atelier — an AI art director for working photographers.**

Working fine-art photographers spend ~30% of their time on grant / residency / competition applications, mostly losing. Atelier removes the writing wall.

Six Claude Opus 4.7 specialist agents (two on Managed Agents) read your portfolio with vision, build a versioned Artist Knowledge Base via a short interview, scout current open calls, score each one for aesthetic fit by comparing your work against past recipients' portfolios with multimodal reasoning, drop the bad fits with explicit reasoning, and draft submission-ready statements / proposals / CVs / cover letters in each program's institutional voice.

The artifact is a Career Dossier — printable PDF and web view — that names what to apply to, what to skip, and why. Saying no with reasons is the product, not a by-product.
